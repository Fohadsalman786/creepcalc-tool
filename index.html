03 · FAQ

Questions & answers

What is freelance scope creep? ▼

Scope creep happens when a client requests additional features, extra revisions, or more meetings beyond the original agreement — without increasing the budget. Your effective hourly rate drops while your time investment increases.

How do I prevent scope creep? ▼

Write detailed contracts with revision caps, set clear deliverables, track time in real-time, and learn to say: "I'm happy to do that — let me send a change order with the additional cost."

Is this really free? ▼

Yes. No login, no signup, no credit card. We keep it free with non-intrusive ads so every freelancer can understand the real cost of unpaid extra work.

Can I share my results? ▼

Yes — click "Share Result" to generate a shareable summary. Freelancers love posting these on Reddit and Twitter to raise awareness about scope creep.
