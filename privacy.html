Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data CreepCalc collects and how we use it.

Summary

CreepCalc does not store, collect, or track any personal data. All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your project details never leave your device.

1. Data We Collect

CreepCalc is designed to operate entirely on the client side. When you enter project details (fees, hours, extra work) into the calculator, that data:

  • Is processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript
  • Is never transmitted to any server or third party
  • Is never stored in any database
  • Disappears when you close or refresh the page

We may collect anonymous usage statistics (page views, session duration) through Google Analytics to understand how the tool is used and improve it.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We use the following services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics — Tracks anonymous page views and user behavior patterns. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Google AdSense — Displays advertisements on our site. AdSense may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing history.
  • Cloudflare — Provides CDN and security services. May collect IP addresses for security purposes.

3. How We Use Data

Any non-personal data we collect is used solely for:

  • Improving the accuracy and features of the calculator
  • Understanding traffic patterns to serve our users better
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites (such as affiliate partners or external tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

5. Data Security

Since we don't store user data, there is no risk of data breaches. However, we implement industry-standard security measures (HTTPS/SSL encryption) to protect the communication between your browser and our servers.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Request information about what data we may hold about you
  • Request deletion of any data we may hold
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Opt out of personalized advertising

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@creepcalc.com
