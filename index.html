03 — FAQ

Common questions

Is this really free? No hidden costs?+
Yes, 100% free. No signup, no credit card, no limits. We monetize through ads to keep the tool free for everyone.
Does the AI Scope Detector actually use AI?+
Yes. It uses a language model to analyze your contract terms against the client's new request and determine if it's within scope. No data is stored.
Can I use the Sign-Off document legally?+
The document serves as evidence of client approval and agreement. It's not a substitute for a legal contract but strengthens your position in any dispute.
Is my data safe?+
Everything runs in your browser. No data is stored on our servers. The AI analysis is sent securely and not retained.
Can I share these tools with other freelancers?+
Absolutely! Spread the word. More freelancers using these tools means less exploitation in the industry.
